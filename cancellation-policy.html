Cancellation & Batch Deferral Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Clear & Fair Policies

At Get Knowledge, we recognize that unpredictable personal emergencies, professional workload, or health events can occasionally disrupt study plans. This policy explains our cancellation and cohort deferral procedures.

1. Cancellation by the Student

  • Before Batch Launch: If you cancel your enrollment prior to the scheduled batch kickoff, refunds are governed strictly by our Refund Policy.
  • Batch Deferral (Free Alternative): Instead of cancelling, students are entitled to a one-time complimentary batch deferral. You can pause your admission and join the subsequent 20-day cohort within 6 months at zero additional charge.

2. Cancellation or Rescheduling by Get Knowledge

While rare, Get Knowledge reserves the right to postpone or cancel an upcoming cohort due to unforeseen circumstances, including mentor medical emergency or severe infrastructure outages.

In the event that Get Knowledge cancels a batch, enrolled students will be offered their choice of:

  1. A 100% full and immediate refund of ₹199 without any deductions whatsoever, processed within 3 business days; OR
  2. A guaranteed priority seat in the next scheduled cohort, plus complimentary 1-on-1 code consultation.

3. Individual Session Rescheduling

If an individual lecture must be rescheduled due to mentor technical difficulties, the missed session will be rescheduled on a mutual slot agreed upon by the cohort. All students will receive advance notice via WhatsApp and email.

4. Missed Classes & Catch-up Support

If you are unable to attend a particular live session:

  • Private lecture recordings are provided for every single class via Google Drive within 12 hours.
  • You can post your code doubts on the batch WhatsApp group for prompt clarification.
