Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Fair & Clear Refund Terms

At Get Knowledge, we are committed to delivering exceptional live learning experiences. Because our live cohort seats are capped at small class sizes to ensure mentor attention, we maintain a clear, fair, and transparent refund policy.

1. Pre-Batch Start Refund Window

If you have paid your course fee (₹199) and wish to cancel your enrollment before the cohort commences:

  • More than 48 hours before Batch Start Date: You are eligible for a 100% full refund of the fee paid.
  • Within 48 hours of Batch Start Date: You may choose to receive an 80% refund or choose to defer your admission to the very next cohort at zero extra cost.

2. First Class Satisfaction Guarantee

We want you to feel confident in your learning. If you attend Lecture 1 (the first live session) and genuinely feel the course does not meet your expectations:

  • You must submit your refund request within 24 hours of the completion of Lecture 1 via email to getknowledge2026@gmail.com.
  • We will process an 85% refund of the fee paid.

3. Non-Refundable Period (After Lecture 2)

Once Lecture 2 of the cohort has commenced:

  • No refunds can be issued as live seats and resources are locked in.
  • If an emergency arises, you may request a Cohort Freeze to join a future batch within 6 months.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to getknowledge2026@gmail.com from the email address you used in your application.
  2. Include the subject line: Refund Request - [Your Name] - AI Development.
  3. Provide your original 12-digit UTR/UPI reference number, date of payment, and the bank account or UPI ID to which the refund should be remitted.
  4. State the reason for your refund request.

5. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is verified by our accounts desk:

  • Refunds will be processed within 5 to 7 business days via direct NEFT/IMPS bank transfer or reverse UPI transfer.
  • A confirmation email containing the refund bank UTR will be sent immediately upon transaction dispatch.
