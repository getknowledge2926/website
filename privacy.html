Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 • Effective Immediately

At Get Knowledge ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our straightforward, transparent practices regarding the collection, use, and safeguarding of information when you visit our website, apply for our online courses via Google Forms, and communicate with our team.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate a user account system, cookie-tracking databases, or automated web scraping tools. We only collect information that you explicitly and voluntarily submit to us through Google Forms or direct email/WhatsApp communication:

  • Course Application Data: Your full name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, selected course (AI Development), and batch timing.
  • Payment Verification Data: Your name, course name, transaction reference number or 12-digit UTR, payment date, and transaction screenshot proof submitted via our Payment Verification Google Form.
  • Direct Inquiries: Any questions or feedback you send to our admissions desk via WhatsApp, email, or our website inquiry form.

2. Information We Never Collect or Store

To ensure utmost security and peace of mind for our students:

  • We NEVER ask for, process, or store your UPI PIN, ATM PIN, net banking login passwords, OTPs, or debit/credit card CVV.
  • We do not store your personal information in website source code or browser local storage.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or sell your contact information to third-party ad networks.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for the legitimate operational purposes of running our online teaching academy:

  • To process and review your course application.
  • To manually cross-verify your payment reference (UTR) of ₹199 against our official bank statement.
  • To send you course welcome kits, Google Meet class links, and syllabus materials via WhatsApp and Email.
  • To provide personalized support, code review feedback, and doubt resolution during the 20-day cohort.
  • To issue verifiable Certificates of Completion upon successful cohort graduation.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To maintain simplicity, security, and low overhead costs, we utilize industry-standard external tools:

  • Google Forms & Google Sheets: Used to securely capture application and payment verification records without managing our own vulnerable database servers. Your data is stored under Google's robust enterprise security infrastructure.
  • Google Meet: Used exclusively to deliver live, interactive lecture sessions.
  • WhatsApp & Email: Used to deliver class updates, doubt resolution, and batch notifications.

5. Data Retention & Security

We retain student enrollment records only for as long as necessary to administer course completion certificates, verify alumnus status, and maintain compliant tax and accounting records. Access to our internal Google Sheets is restricted strictly to authorized academy founders and admissions staff.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of the information we hold about you or request that we update or delete your contact details from our communication lists at any time by emailing us at getknowledge2026@gmail.com.
